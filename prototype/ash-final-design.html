SETTINGS

Try

  • Right Shift or the button above opens and closes the panel; Esc goes back, then closes.
  • Click a tile's name or icon, or its gear, for its options. Crosshair and Hit indicator have full pages, with the colour picker under Colour.
  • The gear at the bottom left is ash's own settings; Interface size resizes the panel live.

Examples, not real

  • Entity culling and Motion blur are not planned features. They're here only to show the green and red FPS marks.
  • Snaplook is shown as if it failed to load, to show the UNAVAILABLE tile and the shake.
  • The game scene, the servers and their player counts, the mods list and the accounts are stand-ins.

Still open

  • Panel darkness now starts at 55%; the slider above still goes 30–65%.
